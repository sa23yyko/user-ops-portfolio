# AGENTS.md

## Project identity
This repository is a personal portfolio project for an education-technology user-operations case study.

## Fact boundaries
- The Gaodun Education market/content-operations internship is a real experience.
- The language-learning-app user-operations case study is a PERSONAL INDEPENDENT SIMULATED PROJECT.
- The 8 interview users and 40 interview responses are simulated.
- The 1000 users and 7306 behavior events are simulated data.
- All SQL funnel, retention, churn, recall, segmentation and A/B-test content based on those datasets must be labeled as simulated.
- Never present any simulated data as internal data from Gaodun, Shanbay, Baicizhan, or any other real company.
- Never invent real product launches, real revenue growth, real retention lift, or real A/B-test results.

## Analysis rules
- Do not silently repair or overwrite the original data.
- Preserve data-quality limitations.
- Distinguish observation, hypothesis, action, and limitation.
- Do not turn correlation into causation.
- Current pain-point statistics use the ORIGINAL 5-category taxonomy from the research files. Finer 6-item breakdowns may appear only as action-level subissues, not as the main statistical taxonomy.

## Visual direction
Priority references are the images in `references/`.

Style:
- Editorial Portfolio
- Playful Minimalism
- Hand-drawn doodle
- Slightly retro printed-paper feeling
- Primary blue: #2461A9
- Cream: #FEF5E2
- Black: #111111
- Large section numbers
- Strong black headings
- Asymmetrical editorial grid
- Thin blue rules / L-shaped lines
- Large whitespace
- Original hand-drawn SVG illustrations

Avoid:
- SaaS-dashboard look
- Excessive rounded cards
- Glassmorphism
- Gradients
- Neon/glow
- 3D effects
- heavy parallax
- stock icon libraries as the primary illustration system

## Motion
Use only restrained motion:
- section fade-up
- count-up numbers
- SVG line draw
- progressive funnel reveal
- 1–3 px doodle floating
- subtle hover lift
- support `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Implementation
- Prefer simple HTML/CSS/JS unless the existing project already uses another stack.
- Keep the site responsive.
- Do not rewrite approved business copy unless explicitly asked.
- Do not re-run broad web research unless explicitly asked.
- Do not re-analyze the full dataset unless explicitly asked.
